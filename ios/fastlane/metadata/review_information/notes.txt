Passport Photo Studio - Froggy Eye Ltd - v1.0.2 - com.froggyeye.passportphotostudio

ABOUT THIS SUBMISSION
An earlier version (1.0.0) was submitted under a different App Store Connect record, which we removed; this is a fresh record for version 1.0.2. Changes since 1.0.0: a new app icon; the background check now tests colour (plain, light, no colour cast) in the corners outside the head; a new neutral-expression check (eight checks in total); and glasses/head-covering rules quoted from each issuing authority. The app, its code and all artwork are original work by Froggy Eye Ltd; no template or purchased code is used.

WHAT THE APP DOES
Pick a country and document (UK passport, US visa and so on), take a photo with the in-app camera or pick one from the library, and the app crops and scales it to that document's published size and resolution. It then runs eight checks with a pass/fail and plain-English reason each: single face, eyes open, looking forward, neutral expression, face size, eye line, background, photo fully captured.

NO ACCOUNT IS REQUIRED
No sign-in or backend; every feature is reachable from a fresh install.

HOW TO TEST (about two minutes)
1. Home shows the selected document; tap it to choose any of the 30 specs. Nothing in the picker is locked.
2. Tap "Take a new photo", or "Pick from gallery" (quicker on a simulator) and choose any photo with a face.
3. The Result screen shows the crop, the spec and the eight-check report.
4. Tap "View 4x6 inch print sheet".
5. Free saves are watermarked and downscaled. A Pro-only action opens the paywall.

IN-APP PURCHASE
One non-consumable, com.froggyeye.passportphoto.pro2 ("Passport Photo Studio Pro"): full-resolution unwatermarked export and the 4x6 print sheet. Price comes from StoreKit. "Restore purchase" is in the Home app bar. The free tier is fully usable.

PERMISSIONS
Camera: to take the photo (optional; gallery import works without it). Photo library read: to pick a photo. Photo library add: to save the result. No other permissions, no ATT prompt.

FACE DATA (answers to the Guideline 2.1 questions)
1. What is collected: nothing. Google ML Kit face detection runs on-device with a bundled model and returns a face bounding box and landmark positions (e.g. eyes) as image coordinates.
2. Use: only to crop the photo to the chosen specification and to run the on-screen checks. Detection, not recognition: no faceprint, no identification, no comparison between photos.
3. Sharing/storage: face data never leaves the device, is never written to disk, never included in saved files, and is not shared with us or any third party. ML Kit's diagnostics to Google contain no face data and no image.
4. Retention: held in memory only while the photo is processed and shown, then discarded.
5. Privacy policy, "Face data" section, https://passportphoto.froggyeye.com/privacy.html - quote: "No face data is collected, stored, or shared. The detection runs entirely on your device against a model bundled inside the app."

PRIVACY AND NETWORK
No photo ever leaves the device; the app has no API client or upload path. Two bundled components use the network: StoreKit, and ML Kit diagnostics (declared in App Privacy as Data Not Linked to You: Device ID, Product Interaction, Performance Data, Other Diagnostic Data; no tracking).

CLAIMS
Neither the listing nor the app claims any authority will accept a photo; only what was measured against a published specification is stated, with a per-spec source list.

CONTENT
No user-generated content, sharing, ads or social features. The face in the screenshots is an illustration drawn by the app, not a real person.
